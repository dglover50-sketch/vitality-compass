# Vitality Compass — Weekly Meal Prep Framework

## Overview

A practical, science-backed meal prep system for busy adults. Three tiers let you start where you are and level up as your confidence grows.

**Philosophy:** Small steps, big impact. No extreme diets, no guilt, no complicated rules.

## The Meal Prep Method: 4 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Choose Your Tier
Pick the category that matches your experience and time availability. If you're new to meal prep, start with 🟢 Beginner and stay there for 2-3 weeks before leveling up.

### Step 2: Shop (Sunday Morning)
Use the shopping list template to buy everything for the week. Stick to the list — this is what saves time and money.

### Step 3: Prep (Sunday Afternoon)
Block 30-90 minutes depending on your tier. Play music or a podcast. The key is to **batch cook ingredients, not meals** — cook versatile components (grains, proteins, roasted veg, dressings) that can be mixed and matched.

### Step 4: Assemble (Daily, 10-20 min)
Each day, quickly assemble meals from your prepped components. Most meals take 5 minutes to put together — just reheat protein, add grain, top with veg and dressing.

## Core Principles

1. **Cook once, eat 3-4 times** — every ingredient you prep should be used in at least 3 meals
2. **Protein first** — always prep your protein source; it's the hardest to cook quickly
3. **Color = nutrients** — aim for 3+ colors on every plate
4. **Dressing is king** — a good dressing makes simple ingredients taste like a real meal
5. **Forgiveness, not perfection** — if you skip a prep day, eat simply. Don't quit
