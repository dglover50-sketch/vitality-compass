# Vitality Compass — Movement & Meal Prep Pairing Plan

A simple weekly movement schedule designed to pair with your meal prep rhythm. Includes pre/post workout nutrition tips for both **vegan and non-vegan** lifestyles.

---

## Philosophy: Fuel, Move, Recover

Your meal prep provides the fuel. Your movement plan provides the stimulus. Together they create a sustainable wellness rhythm.

**Key principles:**
- **Prep day = active recovery** (Sunday is for cooking AND light movement)
- **Hard training days = higher carb meals** (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)
- **Rest days = lighter meals** (your body needs less fuel)
- **Pre/post workout nutrition matters** — timing + right macros

## 3 Workout Variations

Choose the tier that matches your fitness level. The weekly schedule stays the same — only the exercise type, intensity, and duration change.

### 🟢 Beginner: Bodyweight & Basics (~20-30 min/day)

*Focus: Build movement consistency. No equipment needed.*

| Day | Workout | Details |
|-----|---------|---------|
| **Monday** | Full-body bodyweight | 3 rounds: 10 bodyweight squats, 8 push-ups (knees ok), 10 glute bridges, 30-sec plank, 20-sec rest between exercises |
| **Tuesday** | Brisk walk | 20-25 min walk outdoors or on treadmill. Conversation pace. |
| **Wednesday** | Upper body + core | 3 rounds: 10 alternating lunges, 8 incline push-ups (on counter), 10 seated rows (resistance band or no weight), 30-sec side plank (each side) |
| **Thursday** | Yoga / stretching | 15-20 min beginner yoga flow (search: "15 min morning yoga" on YouTube) or static stretching routine |
| **Friday** | Full-body + cardio bursts | 3 rounds: 20 jumping jacks, 10 squats, 8 push-ups, 15 high knees, 20-sec rest. Walk 5 min to cool down. |
| **Saturday** | Fun movement | 30-40 min walk, hike, bike ride, or recreational sport |
| **Sunday** | Active recovery | 15 min gentle stretching or walking while meal prepping |

### 🟡 Maintainer: Light Weights & Cardio (~30-45 min/day)

*Focus: Strength building + cardiovascular fitness. Requires dumbbells (5-15 lbs) or resistance bands.*

| Day | Workout | Details |
|-----|---------|---------|
| **Monday** | Full-body strength | 3 sets x 10-12 reps: goblet squats, dumbbell bench press, bent-over rows, standing overhead press, dumbbell deadlifts. 45-sec rest between sets. |
| **Tuesday** | Jog or cycle | 25-30 min steady-state cardio (jogging, cycling, elliptical). Can hold a conversation but slightly breathless. |
| **Wednesday** | Upper body + core | 3 sets x 10-12 reps: dumbbell chest press, seated rows, lateral raises, bicep curls, tricep overhead extensions. 10 min core: planks, crunches, leg raises. |
| **Thursday** | Active recovery | 20 min mobility flow: cat-cow, hip circles, thoracic rotations, hamstring stretches, pigeon pose |
| **Friday** | Lower body + HIIT | 3 sets x 12 reps: goblet squats, walking lunges, Romanian deadlifts, calf raises. Then 10 min HIIT: 30 sec jump squats / 30 sec rest x 5 rounds. |
| **Saturday** | Fun movement | 40-60 min: hike, bike ride, swim, recreational sport, or dance class |
| **Sunday** | Active recovery | 20 min yoga or gentle stretching. Walk while meal prepping. |

### 🔵 Optimizer: Progressive Overload & Structured Splits (~45-60 min/day)

*Focus: Progressive overload, periodization, and targeted splits. Gym access recommended.*

| Day | Workout | Details |
|-----|---------|---------|
| **Monday** | Push day (chest, shoulders, triceps) | 4 sets x 8-10 reps: barbell bench press, incline dumbbell press, standing overhead press, lateral raises, tricep pushdowns. 60-sec rest. |
| **Tuesday** | Steady-state cardio + abs | 30-40 min zone 2 cardio (running, rowing, cycling at ~135-150 bpm). 10 min core circuit. |
| **Wednesday** | Pull day (back, biceps, rear delts) | 4 sets x 8-10 reps: pull-ups or lat pulldowns, barbell rows, face pulls, seated cable rows, bicep curls. 60-sec rest. |
| **Thursday** | Active recovery | 25 min mobility work + foam rolling. Focus on hips, thoracic spine, and ankles. |
| **Friday** | Leg day + HIIT finisher | 4 sets x 8-10 reps: barbell squats, Romanian deadlifts, walking lunges, leg press. Finish with 10 min HIIT: 40 sec work / 20 sec rest on bike or rower. |
| **Saturday** | Fun or sport-specific | 45-60 min: sport practice, trail run, long hike, or pickle your favorite activity |
| **Sunday** | Active recovery | 20 min yoga + gentle walk. Prep meals for the week. |

## Pre-Workout Nutrition (30–60 min before exercise)

### What to eat
A small meal or snack with **easy-to-digest carbs + a little protein**. No heavy fats or fiber right before — they digest slowly and can cause discomfort.

### Non-Vegan Options

| Snack | Timing | Calories | Why It Works |
|-------|--------|----------|--------------|
| Banana + 1 tbsp peanut butter | 30 min before | ~200 | Quick carbs + steady energy |
| Greek yogurt (½ cup) + berries | 45 min before | ~150 | Protein + carbs, gentle on stomach |
| 1 slice whole grain toast + honey | 30 min before | ~140 | Fast-digesting carbs |
| Small whey protein shake (½ scoop + water) | 45 min before | ~60 | Rapid amino acid delivery |

### Vegan Options

| Snack | Timing | Calories | Why It Works |
|-------|--------|----------|--------------|
| Banana (1 medium) | 30 min before | ~105 | Perfect ratio of fast carbs, zero prep |
| Dates (2–3) + almond butter | 30 min before | ~180 | Natural sugar + healthy fat |
| Oatmeal (½ cup cooked) + maple syrup | 45-60 min before | ~150 | Sustained energy release |
| Small pea protein shake (½ scoop + water) | 45 min before | ~55 | Plant-based amino acids |

## Post-Workout Nutrition (within 30–60 min after)

### What to eat
A meal or snack with **protein (for muscle repair) + carbs (for glycogen replenishment)**. Aim for a 2:1 or 3:1 carb-to-protein ratio.

### Non-Vegan Options

| Snack/Meal | Prep Time | Protein | Why It Works |
|------------|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Chocolate milk (1 cup) | 1 min | 8g | DIY recovery drink — the classic |
| Greek yogurt (1 cup) + berries + honey | 2 min | 20g | Perfect 3:1 carb-to-protein ratio |
| Tuna salad on rice cakes | 3 min | 22g | Lean protein + quick carbs |
| Egg muffin cups (prepped — R9) + toast | 2 min | 18g | Grab from your meal prep, heat and eat |
| Whey protein shake + banana | 2 min | 25g | Fastest possible protein delivery |

### Vegan Options

| Snack/Meal | Prep Time | Protein | Why It Works |
|------------|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Pea protein shake + banana + oat milk | 2 min | 22g | Complete plant protein, fast |
| Tofu scramble (prepped — R9 vegan) + toast | 2 min | 16g | Grab from meal prep, reheat |
| Chickpea salad + pita | 3 min | 15g | Carbs + fiber + protein |
| Soy milk (1 cup) + granola | 2 min | 12g | Convenient, shelf-stable |
| Hummus + whole grain crackers + veg | 2 min | 10g | Easy, no cooking required |

## Meal Prep ↔ Workout Integration

### How Your Prepped Food Supports Your Workouts

| Meal Prep Item | Best Used For | When to Eat It |
|----------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Overnight oats** (R8) | Pre-workout breakfast | 45-60 min before morning workout |
| **Protein smoothie** (R6) | Post-workout recovery | Within 30 min after workout |
| **Mason jar salads** (R2) | Lunch on training days | 2-3 hours after workout |
| **Sheet pan meal** (R1 or R10) | Dinner on training days | Within 2 hours after workout |
| **Egg muffins / Tofu scramble** (R9) | Quick post-workout snack | Right after workout (easy to pack) |
| **Lettuce wraps** (R7) | Lunch on active recovery days | When you want something light but satisfying |
| **Black bean tacos** (R3) | Pre-workout lunch | 2-3 hours before afternoon workout |
| **Grain bowl** (R4) | Dinner any day | Balanced macros for recovery |

### Day-by-Day Pairing Guide

**Monday — Strength Day:**
- **Pre-workout:** Banana + peanut butter (or just banana for vegan)
- **Post-workout:** Greek yogurt + berries (non-vegan) OR pea protein shake (vegan)
- **Dinner:** Sheet pan salmon/chickpea bowl (R10) — carbs + protein for recovery

**Tuesday — Cardio Day:**
- **Pre-workout:** Overnight oats (R8) — sustained energy
- **Post-workout:** Mason jar salad (R2) with extra protein
- **Dinner:** Black bean tacos (R3) — light, plant-forward

**Wednesday — Upper Body Strength:**
- **Pre-workout:** Small smoothie (R6, half portion)
- **Post-workout:** Egg muffin cups (R9 non-vegan) or tofu scramble cups (R9 vegan)
- **Dinner:** Grain bowl with chicken or tempeh (R4)

**Thursday — Active Recovery:**
- **No special fueling needed** — stick to normal meals
- **Great day for:** Mason jar salad (R2) for lunch, veggie stir-fry (R5) for dinner

**Friday — Lower Body + HIIT:**
- **Pre-workout:** Dates + almond butter (or toast + honey for non-vegan)
- **Post-workout:** Chocolate milk (non-vegan) or soy milk + granola (vegan)
- **Dinner:** Lettuce wraps (R7) — lighter but protein-packed

**Saturday — Fun Movement:**
- **Flexible fueling** — match your activity
- **Long hike/walk:** Pack a mason jar salad (R2) + overnight oats (R8)
- **Team sport:** Pre-workout snack + post-workout smoothie

**Sunday — Prep Day + Active Recovery:**
- **15-20 min gentle walk or stretching** — while grains cook, take a walk
- **No special fueling needed** — focus on prepping meals for the week
- **Hydration:** Drink extra water while cooking

## Hydration Tips

- **Pre-workout:** Drink 16-20 oz water 1-2 hours before exercise
- **During workout:** Sip water every 15-20 min
- **Post-workout:** Drink 16-24 oz water for every pound lost during exercise
- **All-day:** Use the meal prep water habit — drink a glass before each meal

### Hydration Boosters (add to water)

| Non-Vegan | Vegan |
|-----------|-------|
| Electrolyte tablets (any brand) | Electrolyte tablets (check for vegan) |
| Coconut water (natural) | Coconut water (naturally vegan) |
| Diluted sports drink | Water + pinch of salt + squeeze of lemon |
