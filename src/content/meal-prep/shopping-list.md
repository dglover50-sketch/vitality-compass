# Vitality Compass — Weekly Shopping List Template

A reusable master grocery list organized by category. Print or copy this template each week, then check off what you need based on your meal plan.

---

## How to Use

1. **Choose your tier** — Beginner, Maintainer, or Optimizer (see meal-prep-framework.md)
2. **Plan your meals** — pick 3–4 recipes from recipes.md for the week
3. **Check the boxes** below for each ingredient you'll need
4. **Shop** — stick to the list to save time and money
