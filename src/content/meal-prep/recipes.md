# Vitality Compass — Recipe Cards

## R1: Sheet Pan Roasted Veg + Protein

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 10 min |
| **Cook time** | 25 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Beginner |
| **Dietary** | Gluten-free, dairy-free |

**Base ingredients:**
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 large sweet potato, cubed (½-inch)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, sliced
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Fresh herbs (dill or parsley)

**Non-vegan option — Salmon:**
- 2 salmon fillets (6 oz / 170g each)

**Vegan option — Tofu:**
- 1 block extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C)
2. Toss sweet potato with 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper — roast 10 min
3. Add your protein (salmon skin-side down or tofu) + broccoli to pan, drizzle remaining oil, season
4. Roast 12–15 min more until salmon flakes or tofu is golden
5. Squeeze lemon, garnish with herbs

**Nutrition highlights (per serving):**
- *Non-vegan (salmon):* Protein: 38g | Fiber: 6g | Omega-3s: 2.5g
- *Vegan (tofu):* Protein: 20g | Fiber: 7g | Iron: 25% DV

**Variations:**
- *Low-carb:* Replace sweet potato with cauliflower florets
- *Gluten-free:* Already GF

## R2: Mason Jar Salad (Customizable)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 15 min (for 3 jars) |
| **Cook time** | 0 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Beginner |
| **Dietary** | Can be made vegan, gluten-free |

**Base formula (per jar, 16 oz wide-mouth):**
Layer from bottom to top:

1. **Dressing** (3 tbsp)
2. **Hard veg** (½ cup) — carrots, bell peppers, cucumber, cherry tomatoes
3. **Protein** (⅓ cup) — see options below
4. **Grain or seeds** (¼ cup) — quinoa, farro, sunflower seeds
5. **Greens** (2 cups) — spinach, romaine, arugula
6. **Topper** (1 tbsp) — nuts, seeds

**Protein options:**
- *Non-vegan:* Grilled chicken, canned tuna, hard-boiled egg, feta
- *Vegan:* Chickpeas, marinated tofu, edamame, toasted pumpkin seeds

**3 Dressing Recipes:**

| Dressing | Ingredients |
|----------|-------------|
| Lemon Tahini | 3 tbsp tahini + 2 tbsp lemon juice + 1 tbsp olive oil + 1 clove garlic + salt + water |
| Balsamic Vinaigrette | 2 tbsp balsamic + 3 tbsp olive oil + 1 tsp Dijon + salt + pepper |
| Greek Yogurt Herb | 3 tbsp Greek yogurt (or vegan yogurt) + 1 tbsp lemon juice + 1 tbsp olive oil + 1 tsp dried dill + salt + pepper |

**To serve:** Shake jar, pour into bowl, enjoy.

**Nutrition highlights (per jar):**
- *Non-vegan (chicken):* Protein: 30g | Fiber: 6g
- *Vegan (chickpeas):* Protein: 18g | Fiber: 10g

## R3: Quick Black Bean Tacos

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 5 min |
| **Cook time** | 10 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Beginner |
| **Dietary** | Vegan (base recipe), gluten-free (use corn tortillas) |

**Ingredients:**
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- ½ tsp cumin
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- 4 small tortillas (corn for GF, flour otherwise)
- Toppings: avocado, salsa, cilantro, lime, shredded lettuce

**Non-vegan add-on:** Top with grilled chicken, shrimp, or crumbled queso fresco
**Vegan add-on:** Extra sliced avocado, pickled onions, vegan sour cream

**Instructions:**
1. Mash black beans with a fork (leave some whole for texture)
2. Heat in a pan with cumin, paprika, garlic powder — 3-4 min
3. Warm tortillas, assemble with toppings
4. Squeeze lime over before eating

**Nutrition highlights (per 2 tacos):**
- *Base (vegan):* Protein: 14g | Fiber: 12g | Iron: 20% DV
- *With chicken:* Protein: 30g | Fiber: 12g

## R4: Lemon Herb Grain Bowl

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 10 min |
| **Cook time** | 25 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Maintainer |
| **Dietary** | Dairy-free, nut-free |

**Base ingredients:**
- 1 cup farro or quinoa (dry)
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon (zest + juice)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt + pepper
- Fresh parsley

**Non-vegan option — Chicken:**
- 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts (6 oz / 170g each)

**Vegan option — Marinated Tempeh:**
- 1 block tempeh, sliced ½-inch thick, marinated 15 min in lemon + garlic + tamari

**Instructions:**
1. Cook farro per package directions (15-20 min)
2. Cook your protein: pan-sear chicken 6-7 min per side (165°F) OR pan-fry tempeh 4 min per side
3. Toss asparagus with oil, salt, roast at 400°F for 10-12 min
4. Serve protein over farro + asparagus, squeeze lemon, garnish with parsley

**Nutrition highlights (per serving):**
- *Non-vegan (chicken):* Protein: 44g | Fiber: 8g | Vitamin K: 80% DV
- *Vegan (tempeh):* Protein: 30g | Fiber: 10g | Iron: 30% DV

## R5: 20-Min Veggie Stir-Fry

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 5 min |
| **Cook time** | 15 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Beginner |
| **Dietary** | Vegan (base recipe), gluten-free (use tamari) |

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups mixed stir-fry veg (bell pepper, broccoli, snap peas, carrot)
- 2 tbsp tamari or soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- Optional: sriracha or chili crisp

**Non-vegan option — Chicken or Shrimp:**
- 1 chicken breast (sliced thin) or ½ lb shrimp — cook first, set aside, then stir-fry veg

**Vegan option — Tofu or Tempeh:**
- 1 block extra-firm tofu, cubed OR 1 block tempeh, crumbled — cook first, set aside

**Instructions:**
1. Cook your protein first (5-6 min), set aside
2. Heat sesame oil in wok on high heat
3. Add garlic + ginger, cook 30 sec
4. Add veg, stir-fry 4-5 min
5. Add tamari + cooked protein, toss to coat
6. Top with sesame seeds. Serve over rice or cauliflower rice

**Nutrition highlights (per serving):**
- *Non-vegan (chicken):* Protein: 35g | Fiber: 5g
- *Vegan (tofu):* Protein: 22g | Fiber: 6g | Calcium: 25% DV

## R6: Protein Smoothie (3 Ways)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 5 min |
| **Cook time** | 0 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Beginner |
| **Dietary** | Can be made vegan or dairy-free |

**Base formula (per serving):**
- 1 cup liquid (see options)
- 1 cup greens or fruit
- 1 scoop protein powder (see options)
- 1 tbsp healthy fat (nut butter, seeds, or avocado)
- Ice cubes

**Non-vegan option:**
- 1 cup milk (dairy) + 1 scoop whey protein + ½ cup Greek yogurt (for creaminess)

**Vegan option:**
- 1 cup unsweetened almond/oat milk + 1 scoop pea/hemp protein + 1 tbsp chia seeds

**3 Flavor Combinations:**

| Flavor | Liquid | Fruit | Protein | Fat |
|--------|--------|-------|---------|-----|
| 🥬 **Green Power** | Milk of choice | 1 banana + 1 cup spinach | 1 scoop protein | 1 tbsp almond butter |
| 🫐 **Berry Bliss** | Milk of choice | 1 cup frozen berries | 1 scoop protein | 1 tbsp hemp seeds |
| 🍫 **Chocolate PB** | Milk of choice | 1 banana (frozen) | 1 scoop chocolate protein | 1 tbsp peanut butter |

**Instructions:**
1. Add all ingredients to blender
2. Blend 30-60 seconds until smooth
3. Adjust liquid to desired consistency

**Nutrition highlights (per serving):**
- *Non-vegan (whey + dairy):* Protein: 30-35g | Calcium: 35% DV
- *Vegan (plant protein):* Protein: 22-28g | Fiber: 8-10g | Iron: 25% DV

**Prep ahead:** Portion dry ingredients into freezer bags — just add liquid and blend

## R7: Lettuce Wrap Bowls (2 Ways)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 10 min |
| **Cook time** | 10 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Maintainer |
| **Dietary** | Gluten-free, dairy-free, low-carb |

**Non-vegan option — Turkey:**
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 cup diced mushrooms
- ½ cup diced water chestnuts
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp tamari
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 8 large butter lettuce leaves
- Toppings: shredded carrot, cucumber, fresh mint

**Vegan option — Lentil-Walnut Crumbles:**
- 1 cup cooked green lentils (or 1 can, drained)
- ½ cup finely chopped walnuts

**Instructions (both versions):**
1. Heat sesame oil in a pan over medium-high heat
2. Cook your protein (turkey OR lentils + walnuts) 5-6 min
3. Add mushrooms, garlic, ginger — cook 2 min
4. Add water chestnuts, tamari, rice vinegar — cook 1 min
5. Serve in lettuce cups with fresh toppings

**Nutrition highlights (per serving):**
- *Non-vegan (turkey):* Protein: 35g | Low-carb: <10g net carbs
- *Vegan (lentil-walnut):* Protein: 18g | Fiber: 10g | Omega-3s: 2g

## R8: Overnight Oats (3 Ways)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 5 min |
| **Cook time** | 0 min (overnight) |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Beginner |
| **Dietary** | Easily made vegan |

- ½ cup rolled oats
- ½ cup milk of choice
- ¼ cup yogurt (dairy or vegan)
- 1 tbsp chia seeds

## R9: Egg Muffin Cups & Vegan Tofu Scramble Cups

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 10 min |
| **Cook time** | 20 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Beginner |
| **Dietary** | Two separate versions for vegan/non-vegan |

### Non-vegan: Egg Muffin Cups
**Ingredients:**
- 6 large eggs
- ¼ cup milk
- ½ cup diced veggies (spinach, bell pepper, mushroom)
- ¼ cup shredded cheese (optional)
- Salt, pepper, dried herbs

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
2. Grease a 6-cup muffin tin
3. Whisk eggs + milk + seasonings
4. Distribute veggies into cups, pour egg mixture over (¾ full)
5. Top with cheese if using
6. Bake 18-20 min until set
7. Cool, refrigerate up to 5 days

**Nutrition highlights (per 2 muffins):**
- Protein: 18g | Vitamin D: 20% DV

### Vegan: Tofu Scramble Cups
**Ingredients:**
- 1 block extra-firm tofu, crumbled
- ¼ cup nutritional yeast
- ¼ cup dairy-free milk
- ½ cup diced veggies (spinach, bell pepper, mushroom)
- ½ tsp turmeric (for color)
- ¼ tsp black salt (kala namak — for eggy flavor)
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
2. Grease a 6-cup muffin tin
3. Crumble tofu into a bowl, mix with nutritional yeast, milk, turmeric, black salt, seasonings
4. Fold in veggies
5. Divide into muffin cups
6. Bake 20-22 min until golden on top
7. Cool, refrigerate up to 5 days

**Nutrition highlights (per 2 cups):**
- Protein: 16g | Fiber: 4g | Iron: 25% DV | Vitamin B12: (from nutritional yeast)

## R10: One-Pan Roasted Veg + Grain Bowl

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Prep time** | 10 min |
| **Cook time** | 30 min |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Beginner |
| **Dietary** | Gluten-free, dairy-free (base) |

**Base ingredients:**
- 1 cup chopped broccoli
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 zucchini, cubed
- ½ red onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- Salt + pepper
- Cooked quinoa or brown rice for serving

**Non-vegan option — Chicken Thighs:**
- 2 chicken thighs (bone-in, skin-on) or 2 breasts

**Vegan option — Chickpea & Avocado:**
- 1 can chickpeas, drained + 1 avocado, sliced (add avocado after roasting)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
2. Toss veg with 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper on a sheet pan
3. Prepare your protein: season chicken with paprika, garlic, oil OR toss chickpeas with same spices
4. Add protein to the same pan (chicken skin-side up)
5. Roast 25-30 min (chicken to 165°F, chickpeas until crispy)
6. Serve over quinoa or rice. For vegan: top with fresh avocado

**Nutrition highlights (per serving, with ½ cup quinoa):**
- *Non-vegan (chicken):* Protein: 38g | Fiber: 7g | Vitamin C: 200% DV
- *Vegan (chickpea + avocado):* Protein: 18g | Fiber: 14g | Healthy fats: 15g

**Variations:**
- *Different veg:* Use whatever's in season — asparagus, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts
- *Grain-free:* Serve over a bed of greens instead
